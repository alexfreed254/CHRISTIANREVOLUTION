# Christ Revolution Movement — Platform Feature Structure

> Complete feature architecture for a modern church website and digital church management platform, divided into the **public website**, **member portal**, and **Super Admin Dashboard**.

This document is the master blueprint for CRM's **Digital Kingdom Ecosystem** — not just a church website, but a centralized environment where people discover Christ, grow spiritually, connect with believers, serve in ministry, access resources, participate in church life, and support the global mission.

---

## Recommended Overall Architecture

```text
PUBLIC WEBSITE
       ↓
MEMBER REGISTRATION / LOGIN
       ↓
MEMBER PORTAL
       ↓
MINISTRY & DISCIPLESHIP ECOSYSTEM
       ↓
SUPER ADMIN DASHBOARD
       ↓
CONTENT + MEMBERS + FINANCE + MINISTRIES + REPORTS
```

1. Public Church Website

4. Discipleship Library
